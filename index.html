Who we are

Surfaces that shape how a space feels.

Core materials

Choose the right core.

Two engineered cores — WPC and SPC — plus PVC and one very deep finish library. Here is how to choose.

Wood Plastic Composite

WPC

Wood fibre fused with polymer for the warm grain and solid feel of timber — no termites, no rot, no sealing. Cuts and screws like wood.

  • Density0.60 – 0.85 g/cm³
  • Profile160 × 24 × 2900 mm
  • Best forFeature walls, living areas, hotels
  • FinishWood grain / matte / embossed
Stone Plastic Composite

SPC

Rigid mineral core that will not warp or bow in tropical heat. The choice where flatness and impact resistance matter.

  • Density1.90 – 2.10 g/cm³
  • Best forLobby, corridor, commercial
  • FinishEmbossed / EIR
Polyvinyl Chloride

PVC

Lightweight, cost-effective panels for ceilings, wet areas and decorative cladding. Water-proof and easy to clean — a practical choice for humid, tropical interiors.

  • Best forCeilings, wet areas, decorative
  • FinishStone / matte / gloss
  • ProfileFlat & fluted
Profile & Finish Library

Profiles

Fluted, great-wall, 3D-carved and flat profiles across hundreds of colours. Custom colour match from a physical swatch.

  • Density8 profile families
  • Best forWalls, ceilings, screens
  • FinishCustom colour on request
